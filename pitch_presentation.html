Overview

Created on June 15, 2025

  • App Name: CO2 Emissions Explorer
  • Purpose: Visualize historical CO2 emissions and predict future trends for any country.
  • Why It’s Cool: Interactive, user-friendly, and educational!
  • Link: Deployed on shinyapps.io (https://etr80a-afnan-khan.shinyapps.io/CO2-Emissions/)

How It Works

  • Input:
    • Select a country from a dropdown menu.
    • Adjust the year range with a slider.
  • Output:
    • Interactive plot showing historical CO2 emissions (blue) and a 10-year prediction (red).
  • Data: Uses the gapminder R package (gdpPercap as a proxy for CO2).

Why Use It?

  • Educational: Learn about CO2 trends across countries.
  • Interactive: Adjust inputs to explore different scenarios.
  • Simple: No advanced knowledge required—just select and view!
  • Try it at: Shiny App Link (https://etr80a-afnan-khan.shinyapps.io/CO2-Emissions/).

Get Started

  1. Visit the app on shinyapps.io.
  2. Choose a country and year range.
  3. Explore the plot and hover for details.
